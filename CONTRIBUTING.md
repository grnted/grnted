# Contributing

Issues are welcome. Open one at https://github.com/grnted/grnted/issues with the command you ran, what Granted
reported and what you expected. For a report made on grnted.co, email hello@grnted.co.

Pull requests are closed for now. When they open, they come with a contributor license agreement (CLA).

## Security

Email security reports to hello@grnted.co, not to a public issue.
