Flu and COVID-19 vaccines

Walk-in vaccine clinics run Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., at 415 Courthouse Square.

Common questions

Who should get a flu shot this year?

Everyone 6 months and older, with rare exceptions. Ask your provider if you have questions.

How much does a vaccine cost?

Most insurance covers vaccines at no cost. No one is turned away for inability to pay.
